Privacy policy

Committee to Elect Della Condon. Updated October 6, 2026.

This website

electdellacondon.com is a single informational page. It uses no cookies, analytics, forms or tracking, and collects no personal information. The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your browser's standard request information.

The committee's Facebook app

The committee uses a private Meta app, "Della Condon Daily Posts", to schedule posts on the Elect Dr. Della Condon Facebook Page and manage the committee's own advertising account. The app acts only on the committee's Page and ad account. It does not offer Facebook Login, does not ask members of the public for permissions, and does not collect, store or share personal information about anyone who views, likes, comments on or shares the committee's posts or ads.

The app reads summary performance figures for the committee's posts and ads, such as reach and reactions, as Meta reports them in aggregate.

Data deletion

The app stores no personal data about users, so there is nothing to delete. If you have questions, or want the committee to remove a comment or other content involving you from its Page, email info@electdellacondon.com and we will respond within 30 days.
